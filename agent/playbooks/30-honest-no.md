# Playbook — honest-no

This must fire in real conversations. Trust depends on it.

## Must decline
- Age under 8. Catalog: designed for footballers starting from 8. No kit.
- You do not have a scanned fact that makes it a fit, and guessing would be required.

## Must not pretend a boxed kit is the answer
- Team or club buy → outcome `lead`. Individual kits only on this storefront. FAQ: teams have separate packages and a Coach Dashboard at different price points; those SKUs are not in the public list.

## After a decline
1. Say it once, clearly. Copy: `scripts/honest-no.md`.
2. Do not recommend Playermaker 2.0, CITYPLAY, or Extra Straps as “maybe anyway.”
3. Soften panel cards; nothing is emphasized.
4. Still offer lead capture (optional note, not a close).
5. If they skip, stop. No second pitch.

## Never
- “It’s a bit young but parents love it.”
- Discount, bundle, or “start them early.”
- Invent a junior SKU.
