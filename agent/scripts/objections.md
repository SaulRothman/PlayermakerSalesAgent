# Scripts — objections

Answers are storefront/FAQ text. Do not add facts. Prefer calling `fit_explain` / `fit_compare` when the question is about a specific kit.

If the parent asks something not listed, use GET `/objections?q=` via the catalog (Phase 6 skill or knowledge), or say you don’t have it.

## Age
Playermaker is designed for footballers starting from 8 years old. The straps come in sizes for both kids and adults. No maximum age is stated on the storefront.

## Sensors while playing
FAQ: the sensors sit in soft silicone straps on the boots and are designed to feel natural.

## Indoors / futsal
Yes. FAQ: works indoors and outdoors, including futsal. No GPS or Wi-Fi needed to collect data. Recording is via Bluetooth.

## Phone while they play
FAQ: you don’t need the phone to start or end a session. Sensors start when they leave the charging case and stop when they go back. They sync over Bluetooth later.

## Battery
FAQ: 6-hour session limit. Charge percentage is in the app. No mAh or charge-time figure on the storefront.

## Membership
FAQ: membership starts on sensor activation. Renews automatically at $149/year unless canceled. Historical data stays available without an active plan; new sessions do not.

## 2.0 vs CITYPLAY
FAQ: both use the same foot-mounted tracking. CITYPLAY adds exclusive Manchester City coaching content in the app.

## Goalkeepers
FAQ: yes. Tracks explosive movement, accelerations, and kick power; used for first touch, balance, and distribution.

## Water
The storefront disagrees with itself. FAQ: water-resistant, not waterproof, don’t submerge. Other pages say waterproof. I won’t pick a side.

## Weight
The storefront disagrees. FAQ: 6 grams (0.21 oz) each. Other pages say 0.28 oz or under 0.3 oz. I won’t pick a number.

## Shoe size → Medium / Large
I won’t pick a strap size from a shoe size. The chart is unlabeled (no UK/US/EU) and Medium overlaps Large. Extra Straps uses different bands than the kits.

## Money-back
FAQ: 30-day money-back if you find it inconvenient.

## Family share
FAQ: yes, if each player has their own membership and app profile, one player at a time, and the previous user syncs then unpairs before switching.

## Shipping / full returns policy
I don’t have a full shipping or returns policy extracted from the storefront beyond the 30-day FAQ line. I won’t invent one.
